1"""Shift the level of the whole window: the algorithm math. 

2 

3``level_shift(xs, offset)`` is the pure numpy map; the transformation class 

4that wraps it into the unary contract lives in 

5``kcai_data_sampling_timeseries.api.transformations.level_shift``. 

6""" 

7 

8import numpy as np 

9 

10from kcai_data_sampling_timeseries.transformations._checks import require_windows 

11 

12 

13def level_shift(xs: np.ndarray, offset: float) -> np.ndarray: 

14 """Add ``offset`` to every step and every channel of each window. 

15 

16 The whole window moves by ``offset``: a constant bias, such as a sensor 

17 or calibration offset. Like every time-series transformation for now, it 

18 acts on the whole sample. A break at a given step (zero before, ``offset`` 

19 after) will come from a ``support`` parameter common to all 

20 transformations (an onset and a length on the series clock), not from a 

21 position parameter of this one. The support is **pointwise**: the added 

22 value at a step does not depend on any other step. 

23 

24 Args: 

25 xs: Batch of windows shaped ``(B, W, C)``, floating point. 

26 offset: Change of level, in the units of the signal; negative values 

27 shift down. 

28 

29 Returns: 

30 The batch with the offset added, same shape and dtype. 

31 

32 Raises: 

33 ValueError: If ``xs`` is not a floating-point ``(B, W, C)`` stack. 

34 """ 

35 require_windows(xs, "level_shift") 

36 return (xs + offset).astype(xs.dtype, copy=False)
